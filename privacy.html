Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

RawConverter does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your photos are processed entirely on your Mac and are never uploaded anywhere by the app.

What the app does with your files

  • It opens only the photos and folders you choose, as allowed by macOS.
  • It saves your edits in small .rawedit files next to your photos and in a library file inside the app’s own storage on your Mac.
  • It writes exported photos only to the folder you pick.
  • It never changes your original RAW files.

No tracking, no accounts, no network

The app contains no analytics, advertising or third-party code, needs no account and makes no internet connections of its own.

Sharing

When you use the Share button, the photos you selected are handed to the service you pick, such as Mail, Messages or AirDrop. That service then handles them under its own privacy terms. RawConverter doesn’t receive or keep a copy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: service@ghsasia.com.
