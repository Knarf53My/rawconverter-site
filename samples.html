Things to try

  1. Unzip the download, open RawConverter and choose File › Open, or drag the folder onto the window.
  2. Select a photo and move the Light sliders, such as Exposure and Highlights. The histogram updates as you go.
  3. On an underwater photo, adjust White Balance, and Dehaze under Detail, to bring back the colors.
  4. Your edits are saved in a .rawedit file next to each photo. The original RAW file is never changed.
  5. Edit › Copy Settings on one photo, then select another and Edit › Paste Settings….
  6. Export as JPEG, HEIC or 16-bit TIFF to a folder of your choice.
