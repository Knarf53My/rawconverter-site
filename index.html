RawConverter

A simple RAW photo converter for the Mac

RawConverter opens the RAW files from your camera, lets you adjust them with familiar sliders and exports them as JPEG, HEIC or 16-bit TIFF. It uses the RAW engine built into macOS, so it’s fast and works with the cameras Photos supports.

  • Light, white balance, color and detail sliders, including Dehaze and Texture
  • Defringe sliders to remove purple and green color fringes
  • A live histogram that warns about clipped highlights and shadows
  • Crop, straighten and rotate
  • An Info window with camera, lens, exposure and location details
  • Copy settings from one photo and paste them onto many
  • Edits are saved in a small file next to each photo, so they travel with your folders
  • Export to any folder, such as Dropbox, or share straight to Mail, Messages and AirDrop
  • Everything happens on your Mac: no account, no cloud, no tracking
